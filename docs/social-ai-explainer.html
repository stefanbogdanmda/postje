Social AI — one-page explainer

A robot intern that runs the social media of small businesses.

Small businesses don't have time to post on Instagram and Facebook every week. Social AI writes the posts for them. The owner just taps approve or reject. Approved posts publish automatically.

The three characters

Who's in the story

👩‍🍳
The client

A small business owner

A bakery, a hair salon, a local shop. Wants a social media presence but doesn't have hours every week to make posts.

🤖
The AI

Claude, the writer

Reads the client's brand, photos, and tone. Writes posts that sound like the business — not like a robot.

🧑‍💻
The operator

Me (the agency)

One person running the platform. Watches all clients from one dashboard. Tunes the AI when a client wants a different style.

The loop, step by step

AI writes the week's posts

3

Client reviews — one tap each

The client opens a simple dashboard. For each post: approve, reject, or skip. No batches, no overwhelm. Five seconds per post.

"Vers uit de oven: ons walnotenbrood…"

4

Approved posts publish themselves

A timer wakes up at the scheduled hour, grabs the approved post, and pushes it to Instagram and Facebook. The client doesn't have to be online.
